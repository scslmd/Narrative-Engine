The Last Lighthouse / Chapter 7: The Conversation
The Conversation
Chapter 7 · Act II · 2,340 words · Last edited 12 min ago

Elara stood in the log room, the AURA-7 drone humming on its charging dock. Its single lens blinked at irregular intervals — the signature of degraded AI.

"The sea... rises," the drone said. "40 meters. You are... the last light."

She paused. The words were fragmented, but the rhythm felt intentional — like a radio tuned between stations, catching signals from somewhere far away.

"Moreau says the inland settlements are failing."

"Moreau... says many things. The data... is not... complete."
